Before you start

🎙️ What gets recorded
Your voice during the session, in browser memory, while we fill in the report. Nothing else.
🗂️ What gets saved
Text and events only: transcript, tool results and the final report. The audio is NEVER uploaded or stored.
🗑️ How it gets deleted
When you close the session the audio is discarded from memory (there is no copy anywhere). The text artifact you can download yourself.
